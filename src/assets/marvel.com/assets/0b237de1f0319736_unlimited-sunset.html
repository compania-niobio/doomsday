1. What's Happening To Marvel Unlimited? ↑

Marvel Unlimited will sunset as of 12/7/2026.  Marvel's licensee WEBTOON is operating a brand-new Marvel digital comics app called "Marvel Comics powered by WEBTOON."

When the Marvel Comics app launches on 11/16/2026, Marvel Unlimited members will be able to import their existing Marvel Unlimited library and reading history to the Marvel Comics app. More details on this process will be shared as launch approaches.


For more information on the sunset timeline and how this impacts your Marvel Unlimited membership, please check out the rest of our FAQs and commonly asked questions below:

 2. Marvel Unlimited Membership & Billing↑

When is the last day I can access Marvel Unlimited? 

As of 10/5/2026, we will no longer be accepting new sign-ups.

As of 12/7/2026, the Marvel Unlimited app will stop having new content added and you will no longer be able to read comics via the app. Members will still be able to browse their library until 12/21/2026 to ensure importing to the Marvel Comics app has been completed successfully.

The Marvel Unlimited app will be removed and sunset as of 12/28/2026.

Existing members as of 10/5 will have their library access continue for Marvel Unlimited – free of charge – until 12/7/2026, with opportunity for Marvel Unlimited subscribers to import their libraries to the Marvel Comics app following the launch on 11/16.

Will my payment information be shared with WEBTOON?

Your existing payment information for Marvel Unlimited will not be shared with WEBTOON. As a result, if you are a current member, you will need to start a new subscription with WEBTOON for access to the Marvel Comics app. You will be able to import your existing library and history as part of the WEBTOON sign-up process. 

To ensure uninterrupted access to Marvel comics content, download the new Marvel Comics app from WEBTOON, available in app stores 11/16/2026. 

Will I be refunded for the remainder of my Marvel Unlimited subscription?   

Yes - all active Marvel Unlimited subscriptions will be eligible for an automated, prorated refund for the remainder of their unused subscription term on or around 11/16/2026.

Please allow 3-5 business days from this date for your subscription refund to be reflected on your chosen payment method.

Members will have their library access continue for Marvel Unlimited – free of charge – until 12/7/2026, with opportunity for Marvel Unlimited subscribers to import their libraries to the Marvel Comics app following the launch on 11/16. 

Need assistance regarding your refund? For subscriptions made through Marvel.com, please contact Marvel Customer Support at help.marvel.com or by phone at 1-844-362-7835 by 12/31/2026. (Hours of Operation: Monday-Friday 8:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. CT). 

For subscriptions purchased directly through Google or Apple, please contact your respective app store for further assistance. 

Note: Marvel cannot assist with billing inquiries where you have signed up through a third party and Marvel is not responsible to you for any charges you incur where you have not cancelled them in accordance with those third party processes. 

3. Marvel Unlimited Annual Plus↑

Will the Marvel Unlimited Annual Plus program continue? 

The Marvel Unlimited Annual Plus program will be retired as of October 5, 2026.

As a thank you to our most dedicated subscribers, active Marvel Unlimited Annual Plus members will receive a Member Exclusive Phoenix Bundle from the Marvel Unlimited team. 

 Unique codes will be sent on or around 10/5/2026 to be redeemed here

While we are saying farewell to the Annual Plus Program, exciting updates are coming with the launch of Marvel Comics powered by WEBTOON. This includes a new Ultimate tier membership, where subscribers can have even earlier access to new releases, one month after the physical release. More updates and perks to be announced soon from WEBTOON!

Please note that Marvel Unlimited Annual Plus Membership bundles may take up to ten (10) weeks to arrive. To update your shipping address, please go to My Account as soon as possible. Marvel only ships Annual Plus Membership Kits to addresses within the United States (excluding U.S. territories and PO boxes). 

Membership kits are subject to availability and Marvel does not guarantee that Annual Plus members will receive the membership bundle featured on the Marvel Unlimited sell page or otherwise advertised at any given time.

I'm not a Marvel Unlimited Annual Plus subscriber. Can I get the final member exclusive bundle? 

Excess inventory from the Member Exclusive Phoenix Bundle will be offered for additional purchase through Marvel.com for eligible Marvel Unlimited annual and monthly subscribers, while supplies last (US only). 

Purchase the Member Exclusive Phoenix Bundle here.

Please note that Marvel Unlimited Annual Plus Membership Kits may take up to ten (10) weeks to arrive. Marvel only ships Marvel Unlimited Annual Plus Membership Kits to addresses within the United States (excluding U.S. territories and PO boxes).

What is included in the Marvel Unlimited Annual Plus Member Exclusive Phoenix Bundle?

To see this year's kit, visit marvel.com/mu-26plus. Please note that the appearance of the actual Marvel Unlimited Annual Plus exclusive limited edition membership kit and contents may vary.

Please note that Marvel Unlimited Annual Plus Membership bundles may take up to ten (10) weeks to arrive. Membership kits are subject to availability and Marvel does not guarantee that Annual Plus members will receive the membership bundle featured on the Marvel Unlimited sell page or otherwise advertised at any given time.

Is the Member Exclusive Phoenix Bundle available outside the U.S.?

Unfortunately, the Member Exclusive Phoenix Bundle is currently available to U.S. residents only.

How long will my Member Exclusive Phoenix Bundle take to arrive?

You can expect to receive your Member Exclusive Phoenix Bundle up to 10 weeks after you place your order. If you have not received your bundle after this time period, please contact us for assistance.

I have not received my Member Exclusive Phoenix Bundle.

If your Member Exclusive Phoenix Bundle has not arrived after the 10-week time frame, please contact us with your email address, shipping address and order number.

What if there is an issue with my Member Exclusive Phoenix Bundle?

If your Member Exclusive Phoenix Bundle has arrived and there is a problem with damaged or missing items, or if your Member Exclusive Phoenix Bundle has not arrived after the 10-week time frame, please contact us with your email address, order number and a description of the issue with your kit.

4. Marvel Comics App Powered By WEBTOON↑

What is the Marvel Comics app?

Coming November 16, 2026, the Marvel Comics app powered by WEBTOON. 

Marvel Comics is a brand-new Marvel digital comics app operated by WEBTOON. It features the same great Marvel digital comics found on Marvel Unlimited, rebuilt for an even better reading experience.

You can learn more about the Marvel Comics app and a founding member offer at marvel.webtoon.com.

Will I be able to access my digital comic collection and reading history via the Marvel Comics app?  

Yes! Your existing Marvel Unlimited library, reading progress, redeemed comics and saved issues can all be transferred over to the Marvel Comics app. To ensure your digital comic collection is accessible via the Marvel Comics app, you will need to create an account with WEBTOON.  Please import your Marvel Unlimited library with the Marvel Comics app prior to 12/7/2026 to be able to validate it migrated successfully from the Marvel Unlimited app.

5. Marvel Digital Comics Redeem Codes↑

How do I redeem my digital copy code?

To redeem your code, go to marvel.com/redeem and enter your code. If your code is valid, the redemption process will continue. As part of the process, you will need to create an account or sign in with an existing account.

Once redeemed, you will see your digital comic in the Marvel Unlimited app within the My Library tab. Redeemed digital comics will have a Purchased label next to them, and you can also filter by Purchased comics within your library.

Stay tuned for updated information on our digital comics redeem codes process when Marvel Comics powered by WEBTOON launches 11/16/2026.

What if my digital copy code is invalid or I can't redeem it?

If your code is invalid when you attempt to redeem or you experienced some type of error that prevents you from redeeming your digital issue, please contact us with the comic title, issue number and original redemption code for further assistance.

Will I need to purchase a subscription to see my library?

You do not need to purchase a subscription to Marvel Unlimited to see your library of purchased and redeemed comics. You also will not need to purchase a subscription to Marvel Comics to see your library of purchased and redeemed comics upon launch on 11/16/2026, but you will need to create an account with WEBTOON.

Will my gifted Marvel Unlimited subscription transfer to WEBTOON? 

More information will be available soon for your existing or unredeemed Marvel Unlimited gift memberships. Stay tuned to your email inbox for more details.
